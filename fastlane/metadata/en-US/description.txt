Bring your FiestaBoard panels to the big screen.

FiestaBoard for Apple TV connects to your own FiestaBoard server and turns your TV into a live display for your messages, dashboards, and pixel artwork.

YOUR PANELS, ON YOUR TV
Browse your available panels and open one as a full-screen display. See updates from your board in an animated split-flap layout or an LED matrix with square or round pixels. Compatible servers can also supply pixel-canvas artwork.

MADE FOR THE REMOTE
Switch panels, return to the panel browser, and adjust display settings with your Apple TV remote. Supported panel settings are shared with your board; TV sizing, calibration, and local display preferences stay with this Apple TV.

EASY CONNECTION
Find compatible boards on your network or enter your server address. On supported servers, approve TV sign-in with a QR code or an activation code, or sign in with your board password. Manage and revoke TV access from your board.

HOME SCREEN PREVIEWS
Move FiestaBoard to the top row of your Apple TV Home screen to see snapshots of your available panels and open a panel directly.

OPTIONAL FIESTABOT
Compatible servers with an AI provider configured can offer FiestaBot to eligible password-based connections. FiestaBot is not available with scoped TV sign-in. AI requests pass through your server to the provider you configure.

REQUIRES FIESTABOARD
This is a companion to a running FiestaBoard server, not a standalone content service. Set up your server and create panels using FiestaBoard's web interface. Virtual panels do not require a physical split-flap display. Network access to your server is required.

Learn more at fiestaboard.app.
