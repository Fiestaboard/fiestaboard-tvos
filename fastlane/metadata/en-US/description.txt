Bring your FiestaPanel displays to the big screen.

FiestaBoard for Apple TV connects to your own FiestaBoard hub and turns your TV into a live display for your messages, dashboards, and pixel artwork.

YOUR PANELS, ON YOUR TV
Browse your panels and open one full screen. Each panel shows as an animated split-flap board or an LED matrix, and note arrays appear as separate Notes. Pixel-canvas artwork from your hub is drawn over the cells. A status chip shows when a schedule, an override, or silence is in charge.

MADE FOR THE REMOTE
Switch panels, return to your panels, and adjust display settings with the Siri Remote. Shared panel settings change the panel on every TV that shows it; TV sizing, calibration, and OLED care stay with this Apple TV.

EASY CONNECTION
Find your FiestaBoard hub on the network or enter its address. Approve this TV from your phone with a QR code or an activation code, or sign in with your hub password. Every TV gets its own sign-in, which you can revoke from your phone or your hub.

OLED CARE
Lower the brightness, move the picture a little, dim at night, and rest the screen on a schedule or when the hub can't be reached.

HOME SCREEN PREVIEWS
Move FiestaBoard to the top row of your Apple TV Home screen to see snapshots of your panels and open one directly.

REQUIRES A FIESTABOARD HUB
This is a companion to a running FiestaBoard hub, not a standalone content service. Set up your hub and create panels with the FiestaBoard app on your phone or on your hub's web page. Virtual panels don't need a physical split-flap display. Network access to your hub is required.

Learn more at fiestaboard.app.
